# 🚀 SPACE LEGACY

### Abandoned but Not Forgotten – Stories of NASA's Equipment on the Moon and Mars

SPACE LEGACY is an interactive educational website that explores the history of NASA equipment left behind on the Moon and Mars.

## 🌌 About the Project

Space exploration leaves behind more than discoveries. Many scientific instruments, spacecraft components, and other equipment remain on the Moon and Mars.

This project tells their stories through an engaging and interactive website.

## ✨ Features

- 🌙 Explore equipment left on the Moon
- 🔴 Discover equipment left on Mars
- 📖 Read detailed equipment stories
- 🕒 Interactive space exploration timeline
- 🧠 Knowledge quiz
- 📚 NASA sources and references
- 📱 Responsive website design

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript

## 🎯 Project Objective

To educate people about the history of space exploration and the equipment that remains on the Moon and Mars through interactive storytelling.
